The approach

Home technology got a reputation for being complicated. That was an installation problem.

Technology at home should make life simpler. One button turns on the TV and everything it needs. A glance at your phone shows the kids in the backyard. We start by asking what you want, build exactly that, and leave room to add more later.

Convenience first

One tap: garage open, alarm off, lights on, music playing.

Some clients want control of everything from a single app, and a button by the door that sets the whole house for the evening.

Security first

See who is at the door, and know the garage is closed.

Others want certainty: live video of the front door, a text if the power fails and another when it is restored, proof that every door is shut.

Residential systems

8 disciplines, one team

01

Security & Surveillance

Monitored burglary, fire, carbon monoxide and flood protection, with cameras placed from a survey of your property. We install and service thousands of systems across New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

  • Honeywell
  • Resideo
  • Kidde
  • Hanwha
  • Cellular radio
  • Smoke, heat, CO, glass break, motion, door, window, temperature and flood sensors
  • Hardwired, wireless or a mix, chosen room by room
  • Cellular communication, more reliable than phone or internet lines
  • Battery backup in the panel for power outages
  • Hardwired IP cameras to a local NVR, or Wi-Fi cameras where wiring is impractical
  • Doorbell cameras with instant alerts and two-way talk

Placement matters. A smoke sensor within three feet of a supply duct collects dust and trips false alarms. Details like this are why we install every system ourselves.

02

Keyless Entry & Access

Lock, unlock and check every door from your phone. Let in a contractor while you are away, and know the moment the kids are home from school.

  • Deadbolts
  • Levers
  • Keypad codes
  • App control
  • Deadbolt and lever styles in finishes to match your hardware
  • Keypad codes for family, guests and service companies
  • A notification for every lock and unlock
  • Garage door status and control in the same app
  • Backup battery terminal if the lock's batteries run out

Remote control relies on your internet connection. The keypad at the door works either way.

03

Home Automation

Control4 ties lighting, climate, shades, audio, video and security into one system, run from keypads, touchscreens, your phone or your voice. It is the control platform we recommend and support, because it stays simple for the people who live with it.

  • Control4
  • Lutron
  • Honeywell
  • Resideo
  • Connected thermostats with schedules and remote adjustment
  • Automatic setback at night and back to comfort by morning
  • On-screen control of climate and security from any TV
  • Scenes that combine lights, shades, music and locks
  • Expandable as your needs change

Check with your HVAC contractor first: some systems need an extra module to work with a connected thermostat.
