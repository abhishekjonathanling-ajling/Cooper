The field, then and now

The technology changed. The fundamentals did not.

Over four decades the industry moved from copper lines and analog tape to cellular radios, IP video and apps. We installed every generation and kept what worked.

Wired panels and keypads

Hardwired alarm panels, keypads and phone-line dialers. Systems were judged by how well they were installed, and the best of them ran for decades.
